## When generation becomes a rollout
A convincing short clip does not guarantee a coherent long sequence. Reusing generated history can accumulate errors in motion, identity, and response to controls.

## From visible errors to internal state
This research studies causal text-to-video and action-conditioned generation across increasing rollout lengths. Probes examine VAE latents, transformer hidden states, and key-value histories. Comparisons between online, refreshed, and recomputed histories help separate visible drift from memory effects.

Partition-consistency post-training explores how to reduce errors across rollout boundaries while preserving short-horizon quality.
