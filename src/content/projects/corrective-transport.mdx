## The question
Reward optimization and few-step distillation often optimize different objects. This work asks whether both can be formulated around the finite-step operator actually used at inference.

## The idea
A corrective transport field acts alongside a frozen base generator. The solver rule stays fixed, while the correction is conditioned on the evaluation budget. Terminal improvement provides a signal for pathwise optimization, and a conditional distribution-matching objective connects the result to target data.

The research covers conditional generation and restoration. It combines reinforcement learning, distillation, and finite-step optimization.
