## Generating structured market sequences
Limit order books contain multiple price levels and a temporal history. LoBiFlow conditions a flow-matching generator on that history to model structured market sequences.

The public repository includes chronological dataset preparation, model implementations, and training and evaluation entry points. The thumbnail is a schematic order book, not a generated market sample.
