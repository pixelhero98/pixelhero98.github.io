## How much information survives propagation?
Hidden width and depth determine how a graph neural network represents and compresses signals. Choosing them only by convention can leave the architecture poorly matched to the graph.

## A capacity-based view
C3E models spectral graph propagation as a communication channel and formulates architecture selection as a constrained optimization problem. It connects graph structure, propagation, and representation size.

## Reading the demonstration
A small path graph carries eight orthogonal signals. Lazy graph diffusion attenuates its spectral modes; width limits how many channels are kept. The displayed Gaussian-channel capacity proxy is calculated for this toy system. It is not the complete C3E estimator, and it does not equate over-squashing with over-smoothing or claim that every graph behaves this way.
