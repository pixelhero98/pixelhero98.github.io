## Relational and individual structure
Stocks carry individual temporal features as well as changing relationships to other stocks. Repeatedly interleaving transformations with graph propagation can make those roles hard to separate.

## Decoupled representation learning
DGDNN builds dynamic graphs using entropy-driven edge generation, learns dependencies through graph diffusion, and separates the representation-learning stage from relational propagation. The extended publication develops the same research direction.

## Reading the demonstration
Start with six signed features, apply a fixed nonlinear encoder once, then diffuse its output over a ring. Controls vary the encoder gain and the number of diffusion steps. This is an operational illustration of decoupling, not the trained encoder, learned graph, or full DGDNN architecture.
