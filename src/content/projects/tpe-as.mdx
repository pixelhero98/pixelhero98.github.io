## Search under a limited budget
An expensive evaluator may produce an attractive score by chance. Optimization must balance high observed utility against uncertain outcomes while spending a finite number of evaluations.

## An adaptive objective
TPE-AS augments Parzen-density search with a budget-dependent mean–variance objective and clipped importance correction. The public implementation provides reusable optimizer mechanics and synthetic benchmarks.

## Reading the demonstration
The toy objective has a broad, stable peak and a narrow, noisy peak. A fixed, seeded set of evaluated candidates is ranked by sample mean minus a scheduled variance penalty. Move the budget-progress control to see the selected incumbent change. This isolates adaptive scoring; it does not implement Parzen proposal generation, importance correction, or a financial backtest.
