## More than one kind of relationship
Stock relations vary over time and can depend on the horizon at which they are measured. A single static relation graph cannot capture all of that structure.

## Diffusion and retained representations
MGDPR constructs multiple stock graphs, refines relations through diffusion, and combines diffused features with retained representations. Parallel retention helps connect relational context to distinctive node features.

## Reading the demonstration
Two hand-built relation graphs carry a synthetic scalar signal. Toggle either graph to inspect its contribution, then blend the diffused signal with the original features. This linear blend illustrates the separate branches; the paper's learned parallel-retention layer uses projections and interactions rather than this scalar blend. No stock returns or predictions are shown.
