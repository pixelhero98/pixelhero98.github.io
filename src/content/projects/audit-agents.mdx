## Answers with an evidence trail
Audit questions require more than fluent prose. An answer must identify its supporting sources, perform calculations consistently, and refuse when the evidence is insufficient.

## Research and public software
The EPSRC project studies restricted-domain audit agents, parameter-efficient post-training, and programmatic evaluation. Public AuditOps provides related corpus, evidence, validation, and benchmark foundations.

The private research programme and the public repository have different scopes. Private datasets and research performance are not represented as capabilities of the public software.
