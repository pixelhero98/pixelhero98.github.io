This blog brings together notes on generative modelling, reinforcement learning, and optimization. The aim is to make the connection between a mathematical formulation and its implementation explicit.

A useful explanation should answer three questions:

1. **What is the claim?** State the result and the assumptions under which it holds.
2. **Why does it hold?** Work through the argument, including the steps that are easy to skip.
3. **What changes in code?** Use a small experiment to check the reasoning and identify where approximations enter.

Topics will include continuous-time dynamics, transport and density evolution, efficient generative inference, and learning objectives. Some posts will be short explanations of a single equation; others will connect a derivation to an implementation.

Research projects and publications are collected on the [research page](/work/) and [papers page](/publications/). This space is for the explanations and experiments around them.
